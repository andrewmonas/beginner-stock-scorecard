How the score works

No secret formula. Every number below comes straight from the code that computes the scores, so this page always matches what the site actually does.

The big picture

  1. Every weekday evening, after the US stock market closes, a small program downloads daily closing prices for about 100 of the largest US companies and a few major index funds.
  2. For each one it measures a few things about the past: its trend, how it did compared with the market, and how calmly its price moved.
  3. Each part becomes a number from 0 to 100. The total score is a weighted average of the parts.
  4. The score becomes a label: .
The score only looks backwards. Markets react to news nobody can predict, so a “Looks strong” stock can fall sharply tomorrow. Treat it as a starting point for learning, never as a reason on its own.

The weights

How each part is scored

Each measurement is turned into 0 to 100 with a straight-line scale between two anchor points. Anything past an anchor is capped at 0 or 100. For example, if “10% below” scores 0 and “10% above” scores 100, then exactly at the average scores 50 and 5% above scores 75.

Which stocks are included

Only large, well-established companies (about the 100 biggest in the S&P 500) and major index funds like SPY, QQQ and VTI. No penny stocks, no meme stocks, no crypto. A stock needs at least one year of price history to be scored.

Where the data comes from

  • Prices: daily closing prices from Alpaca Markets (IEX feed), adjusted for stock splits and dividends.
  • Smart money (optional): insider trades (SEC Form 4) and congressional trade reports from Quiver Quant.

Known limits

  • Past prices can't see future news: earnings surprises, lawsuits, recessions.
  • Trend and momentum signals often fail when the market suddenly changes direction.
  • The IEX feed covers a slice of all US trading. Closing prices can differ by a few cents from other sources.
  • No company fundamentals (profits, debt, valuation) are included.
  • The track record is the honest test of whether any of this works.
