How to read this

  • Group return: the average return of all stocks labelled “Looks strong” that day, counted equally, from that day's close to the close 21, 63 or 126 trading days later (about 1, 3 and 6 months).
  • S&P 500: the return of SPY over the same days.
  • Difference: group minus S&P 500. Positive (green) means the group did better; negative (red) means worse.
  • Trading costs and taxes are not included. Overlapping periods mean nearby days are not independent results, so even a long record of “beat the market” can be luck.
  • Tracking started on launch day. Nothing before that date is recreated or adjusted.
